Privacy

This tool processes authorization credentials, selected account records, and operational logs to perform actions requested by its operator.

Data may be exchanged with the account provider and an operator-configured notification service as needed for those actions. It is not sold or used for advertising.

The operator can stop the tool, revoke authorization through the account provider, and delete locally stored credentials and logs.

These information pages contain no account data, sign-in forms, or tracking scripts. A hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs.
